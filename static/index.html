Walk-in queue management

Take a number.
Take a walk.

QueueLess replaces the paper ticket and the crowded waiting room. Customers scan once, leave, and come back when their phone says it's time. Staff call the next person with a single key.

Take a number → Open the display board
01

Estimates that learn

Wait times come from how long each service is actually taking today, not a fixed guess. Every estimate is an honest range with a confidence level.

02

Leave the line

Phones buzz when three people are ahead, and again when it's their turn, with the counter to go to. Nobody has to stand and watch a screen.

03

Fair by design

Priority customers get a ten-minute head start, not a permanent one. A missed call moves to the back once, then expires, so the line keeps moving.
