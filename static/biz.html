Your door sticker

  1. Generate your business's own QR code. It only ever opens your page — your menu, your orders.
  2. Print the sticker and put it on your door or counter.
  3. Customers scan, order and pay (card now or cash at pickup). Orders appear on this screen.

Lost a sticker or want to stop an old one? Generate a new code — every older sticker stops working straight away.

New orders play a sound. Start the screen to turn sound on.
